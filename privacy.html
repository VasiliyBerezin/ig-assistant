Privacy Policy — Vasya Claude Assistant

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Vasya Claude Assistant is operated by Vasilii Berezin, Paris, France. Contact: vasiliyberezin.art@gmail.com.

What data we access

Through the Instagram Graph API, and only for Instagram professional accounts whose owners have authorised the app: profile information, media and their insights, comments on that media, and direct messages sent to the account (sender ID, username, message text, timestamps).

How we use it

Solely to let the account owner read and answer their own messages and comments and view their own statistics. Replies are only sent by the account owner, within Instagram's 24-hour messaging window.

Storage

Data is fetched on demand and is not stored in any database. Access tokens are kept encrypted-at-rest on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Nothing is retained beyond the working session. Users can revoke access at any time in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites, and request deletion as described on the Data Deletion page.

Your rights (GDPR)

You may request access, correction or deletion of any data concerning you by writing to the contact address above. We answer within 30 days.
