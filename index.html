Vasya Claude Assistant

A private assistant tool used by artist Vasilii Berezin (Paris) and the non-profit Association Microcrédit to manage their own Instagram professional accounts: read and reply to direct messages, moderate comments and review account insights from one desktop workspace.

The tool only accesses Instagram accounts whose owners have explicitly logged in and granted permission. It does not post content, does not message anyone who has not written first, and does not sell or share data.
